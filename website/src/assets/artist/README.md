# Artist sprite sheet

These files are the site owner/artist's supplied visual assets for the Legacy of Goku Hub redesign. They are project-specific artwork, not third-party game art.

Source: `legacy-of-goku-hub-complete-agent-visual-package.zip`, `04-interactive-trial/assets/` (prepared from the artist's originals in `02-artist-originals/`). The 64x64 ball sprites are the transparent crops supplied for UI use; the large coloured/grayscale source rows were not added because their backgrounds are opaque.

`energy-banner-960x160.png` is the supplied Kamehameha-style energy strip. It is the site masthead, shown at its native 960x160 on screens 1032px and wider and scaled down to fit below that. The site name is live text placed in its white core until the artist's lettering is supplied.

The ball sprites are 64x64 files with a 48x48 ball that does not sit in the centre of the canvas. `Ball.astro` shows only the 48x48 ball so lit and grey balls line up. The files themselves are unchanged.
